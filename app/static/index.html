What can you build from your pile?

Spread your bricks out, take a photo from above, and lego.jplay identifies each piece, finds official sets you can (almost) build, and suggests original builds.

For the best results

  • Plain background that contrasts with your bricks: a grey or dark sheet works for most colours.
  • No touching: leave a gap around every piece. Touching pieces are counted as one.
  • Straight above, evenly lit, no harsh shadows or flash glare.
  • Fill the frame, and keep the table edge out of the photo.
  • Tiny parts? Take a closer photo of just those.
